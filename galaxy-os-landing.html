What Galaxy Does
Galaxy handles the full operational life of your organization:
  • Attendance — who showed up, who was absent, trends over time
  • Approvals — leave requests, budget requests, purchases, any decision that needs sign-off
  • Financial records — offerings, dues, donations, contributions, and expenses
  • Member management — profiles, contact info, groups, status, and history
  • Welfare tracking — who needs a follow-up, who hasn't been seen in a while
  • Reporting — weekly, monthly, and custom reports generated automatically
  • AI insights — patterns and suggestions surfaced without you having to ask
Members simply send a message in your WhatsApp group — "I'm here," "Present," or even just their name — and Galaxy captures it as attendance. For in-person events, you can also use a check-in link or QR code. Galaxy then compiles this automatically into an attendance report with member names, percentages, trends, and flagged absences. No manual counting, no spreadsheets, no register to maintain.
When someone sends a request in a WhatsApp group — "I need Thursday off" or "We need ₦80,000 for the event" — Galaxy automatically recognizes it as a request and turns it into a structured approval. The right person is notified with the request details and simple Approve/Decline options, all within WhatsApp. Once decided, Galaxy logs the outcome, notifies the requester, and records everything with a timestamp. Nothing gets lost. Everything is auditable.

Privacy & Safety
Your organization's data belongs entirely to you. Galaxy uses bank-grade encryption, and your data is never shared with other organizations or used for advertising. Only the people you authorize within your organization can access your records. We comply with data protection regulations and store data on secure servers. You can export or delete your data at any time.
Galaxy only processes messages in the specific groups you've connected to it. It doesn't access any other WhatsApp conversations, personal chats, or groups you haven't authorized. Within connected groups, it reads messages to identify organizational activities (like attendance, requests, and approvals) and ignores regular conversation. Think of it like a very attentive admin who only listens for the things that matter to running your organization.
